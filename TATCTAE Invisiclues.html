Orientation

Why do I need an orientation?
1.Time: All Things Come to an End is not quite like any other IF title you may have played.
2.You have less freedom than usual.
3.TATCTAE (oof) is almost entirely linear.

What does that mean, in this case?
1.-Your adventure will take you through a collection of shorter scenes; you'll know when you've finished each.
2.But you may not have actually beaten them.
3.Actions in one short section can doom you to death immediately, in the next section, or in almost any later section.
4.These actions can include missing items, taking items, holding items, leaving items in the wrong place, disturbing your environment, *not* disturbing your environment, making noise, emitting light, having an item in your rucksack at the wrong time...
5.BIG spoiler coming next, so...
6.In one case, but thankfully only one, you lose by not setting up a stable time loop.

How do those item questions work?
1.Usually, the first hint will explain where to get an item, in general terms.
2.The second will be more specific.
3.The third will be explicit.
4.The fourth hint will explain where and when and how to use an item, in general terms.
5.The fifth will give a specific time and/or place.
6.The sixth will be very direct.
7.The seventh will cover any other important information.
8.The questions focus on 'missable' items - that is, items that you can't necessarily backtrack for.
9.This is necessary due to the fact that many items are found in one time and place and used in another.
10.And more importantly, due to the fact that the player might like to know whether they can drop an item after using it once.

Zarf?
1.Check the length of this file.
2.Check the number of topline questions about getting stuck or lost.
3.Check the list of ways to make the game unwinnable under Postgame. Or don't, spoilers are bad.
4.Sometimes Nasty, sometimes Cruel.
5.For more specific details that might alleviate a certain amount of suffering, read below.
6.In almost all cases, points of no return are evident.
7.The game is horribly inconsistent about clearly delineating scenery from objects. But, *generally*, you need not refer to completely unmentioned things.
8.There are many timed sections requiring a precise sequence of moves.
9.There are many missable items, some more or less obvious than others.
10.There are many largely unprompted commands required to finish the game, often in connection with the above.
11.Deaths are *generally* relatively close in time to the actions that trigger them. But unfortunately for you, not always.
12.No game phenomena are nondeterministic. Not that this hinter knows of, at least.

Miscellaneous

1.It varies widely.
2.As a rule, no, but it's very unpredictable.
3.In the early portion of the game, your inventory space is finite, but you can still manage to carry everything you need.
4.You'll soon find something to fix this.
